# Initial Paper-Trading Focus

PolyTrader will initially concentrate paper execution and research on liquid Polymarket binary markets with complete public yes-token books, frequent updates, bounded spreads, and observable lifecycle status. This keeps the first loop measurable and makes book-walking fills meaningful.

The expansion order is deliberate: establish replay and fill trust first, then add event studies and technical features, then broaden to fundamentals, macro regimes, cross-asset exposure, and adaptive allocation. No signal is presumed profitable; expansion requires reproducible evidence, calibration, and risk review.
