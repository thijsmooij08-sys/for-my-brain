# Research standards

No strategy is trusted because it trades or wins. Future research needs point-in-time data, fees/spread/slippage, temporal train/validation/test separation, calibration, reproducibility, and out-of-sample evidence. A single result cannot promote a strategy.

Intelligence outputs are features or evidence, not automatic trading rules. Technical indicators, DCFs, insider activity, macro regimes, earnings surprises, and event patterns require objective definitions, point-in-time data, minimum sample size, holdout or walk-forward validation, uncertainty intervals, stability checks, multiple-comparison protection, and net-of-cost results before strategy use. Composite rankings retain component scores and disagreement.

Pattern discovery additionally requires a declared hypothesis, dataset and
sample size, effect size, uncertainty interval, temporal validation result,
out-of-sample result, cost-adjusted result, stability, and status. Multiple
comparisons require an appropriate correction or false-discovery-rate control;
a low in-sample p-value is not promotion evidence.
