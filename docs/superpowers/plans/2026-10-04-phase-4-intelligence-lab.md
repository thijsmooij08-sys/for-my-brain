# Phase 4 ExecPlan — Intelligence Lab

## Objective

Build evidence-backed research engines over the verified Phase 2 data and Phase 3 simulation foundation, beginning with Polymarket event/technical intelligence and keeping fundamentals, valuation, macro, and peer engines provider-isolated.

## Scope

Feature contracts, technical features, event studies, pattern discovery with multiple-testing controls, Polymarket event graph, scenario/exposure inputs, and explainable signal components. No engine may execute, size, approve risk, or imply profitability.

## Milestones

- P4-A canonical intelligence feature contracts and versioning
- P4-B technical/event-study engines over point-in-time datasets
- P4-C pattern discovery and research significance reports
- P4-D Polymarket event graph and exposure inputs
- P4-E adversarial review and Phase 1–3 regression verification

## Gate

Every output must carry evidence identifiers, as-of timestamp, feature/model version, confidence, limitations, and disagreement metadata. Strategy, risk, execution, accounting, and live-order boundaries remain unchanged.

## Progress

P4-A through P4-D are implemented: evidence-backed technical/event/valuation/fundamental/peer/earnings/macro/income outputs, pattern discovery with false-discovery control, signal fusion with disagreement, and Polymarket event graph relationships. P4-E remains the final full verification gate.
