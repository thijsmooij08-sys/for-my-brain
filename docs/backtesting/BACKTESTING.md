# Backtesting (future)

Future backtests must be point-in-time correct, reproducible, and aware of fees, spread, liquidity, slippage, fills, and latency. No future resolution, price, or clarification may leak into a historical decision.

Research simulation must retain dataset/vintage identifiers, event timestamps,
hypotheses, sample sizes, holdout or walk-forward splits, multiple-testing
controls, bootstrap intervals, stability results, and cost-adjusted outcomes.
